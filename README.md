# Fundamental Equity and Special Situations Research

Historical fundamental research on corporate separations, holding-company discounts,
capital allocation and event-driven situations. The research asks who is selling,
what the price implies, which events could close the valuation gap, and what would
invalidate the thesis.

The July 2–13, 2026 archive contains **185 graded entries across 19 research domains**
and **10 company or thematic dossiers**. Companies can appear in several domains;
185 is an entry count, not a count of unique securities. The archive includes
rejected hypotheses and adverse outcomes. It contains no executable valuation
model, trading system, or verified performance record.

## Reading the archive

Start with [WEX](ideas/wex.md) for the distinction between compensation awards and
insider purchases, [Burford](ideas/burford.md) for the difference between portfolio
realizations and cash receipts, or [Vivendi](ideas/vivendi.md) for a legal catalyst
that failed. The [backlog](ALPHA_BACKLOG.md) contains the screening methods,
original priorities, historical event calendar and rejected hypotheses.

Each dossier covers the situation, seller incentives, business quality, earnings
and balance sheet, valuation scenarios, catalysts, failure conditions, verdict
and remaining verification work. Read its dated **audit disposition** before
using the original verdict. July labels and price thresholds are historical
research classifications, not orders or evidence of executed positions.

## What the audit changed

The October review corrected selected primary-source inputs and their downstream
arithmetic, including Tenet's recurring cash flow after distributions to minority
owners, FICO's earnings and stock compensation, WEX's segment earnings and fuel
assumptions, Hyundai's outstanding share count, and trust distribution mechanics.
It also corrected scenario returns, valuation bases and unsupported entry/sizing
claims, and reconciled affected backlog entries. Dated notes in each dossier
explain the inputs, calculations, sources and unresolved questions.

The original research's claim that 164 initial candidates had been verified is
not supported by a claim-by-claim evidence register and is not a validated coverage
statistic. Previous arithmetic corrections and the October audit do not establish
that every market, legal, policy or company claim is correct.

## Dates and evidence

These remain **historical July 2026 research drafts**, with later corrections.
Prices, expired deadlines and original labels have not been comprehensively
refreshed to October 2026. Later evidence is identified separately so that it does
not become information supposedly available at the original research date.

Selected primary disclosures were checked, but other sources remain in prose,
secondary reports, estimates or open verification lists. Valuation scenarios are
conditional illustrations: internally consistent arithmetic does not establish
that their inputs, probabilities or downside floors are reliable. No executed
positions or realized investment returns are documented. These notes are not
current investment advice.
