# Fundamental Research

Fundamental equity research on corporate separations, holding-company discounts,
capital allocation and event-driven situations. The research asks who is selling,
what the price implies, which events could close the valuation gap, and what would
invalidate the thesis.

The July 2026 archive contains **185 candidates across 19 research domains** and
**10 detailed company or thematic dossiers**. It includes rejected ideas and
adverse outcomes as well as positive theses.

## Outcomes and corrections

The archive records these outcomes through July 13, 2026:

- **Vivendi:** the PURSUE case depended on a July 8, 2026 court ruling on control.
  The ruling went the other way, the tender-offer catalyst failed and the entry was
  downgraded to C. See the [postscript](ideas/vivendi.md#postscript--outcome-2026-07-13).
- **Burford and WEX:** the backlog hypotheses that prompted each deep dive were
  rejected. Burford traded at a premium to book value excluding YPF, so the
  litigation upside was not free. The WEX insider "purchases" in Form 4 filings were
  bonuses paid in stock, not open-market buys. Both names stayed on WATCH for
  valuation reasons. See the [Graveyard](ALPHA_BACKLOG.md#graveyard).
- **Backlog events:** Two Harbors shareholders approved the $12.00 deal with no
  price increase, and the GDV activist catalyst ended when all three of the
  manager's nominees were elected.

Corrections made for this edition are marked in the text where they appear:

- [Burford](ideas/burford.md#4-normalized-owner-earnings--balance-sheet-arithmetic-shown)
  (§4 and §8): owner earnings at $600M of realizations, multiples of book value
  excluding YPF, and the $2.75 trigger, which is about 1.14x deployed cost rather
  than 0.9x.
- [Listed PE and investment trusts](ideas/listed-pe-trust-cluster.md#4-normalized-owner-earnings--balance-sheet-arithmetic-shown-unverified-inputs-flagged)
  (§4 and §8): HVPE tender acceptance and buyback accretion, and an EOT entry
  ceiling of about 910.7p before other costs.
- [Stratus](ideas/stratus.md#4-normalized-owner-earnings--balance-sheet-arithmetic-shown-unverified-inputs-flagged)
  (§4): the low-case cost bridge leaves $104–122M before taxes, short of the
  ~$140M requirement.
- [Vivendi](ideas/vivendi.md#5-valuation-what-the-price-implies-and-base--bull--bear-intrinsic-value-range)
  (§5 and §7): the bear range becomes €1.15–1.40, about 36–47% downside rather
  than 20–25%, and expected value falls to about the entry price.
- [UHS and Tenet](ideas/uhs-thc-healthcare.md#5-valuation-what-the-price-implies-and-intrinsic-value-ranges)
  (§5): §4 models only partial losses of supplemental payments, so the price is
  no longer described as covering a full loss.
- [Mobility Global](ideas/mobility-global.md#6-catalyst-map-dated-where-possible)
  (§6, with the §9 checklist updated to match): the first 13F snapshot after the
  spin-off is September 30 holdings, due November 16.

These corrections weaken several original verdicts. The affected sizing and entry
thresholds have not been re-underwritten, and none of these outcomes is a
performance record.

## Dates and evidence

These are **historical research drafts from July 2–13, 2026**. Prices, event
calendars, position-sizing examples and PURSUE/WATCH/PASS labels refer to the dates
in each note. Later updates within a dossier take precedence over its initial
verdict and the backlog.

Market facts have not been comprehensively revalidated for this edition. Selected
primary-source links have been checked; some sources are still described in prose
rather than linked, and the original open verification items remain. Arithmetic
and reporting-date corrections are identified where they affect the analysis.
The notes do not establish realized investment
returns or a verified forecasting record, and are not current investment advice.
