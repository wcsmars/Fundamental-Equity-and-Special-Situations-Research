## 1. Situation in three sentences

Korea's Third Commercial Code Amendment (passed late Feb 2026, promulgated and effective **March 6, 2026**) makes cancellation of treasury shares the default — within one year of acquisition, and within 18 months (by **~September 6, 2027**) for legacy blocks — which triggered the largest cancellation wave in Korean history: SK Inc will cancel **14,694,388 shares (20.1% of issued) on January 4, 2027**, Samsung C&T is completing the retirement of its original 13.2% treasury block, and 100+ companies resolved ₩15.8tn of cancellations in the March AGM season alone. The catch the backlog entry missed: the SK and Samsung C&T catalysts are **announced, dated, and largely priced** (SK Inc +205% and Samsung C&T +136% over the past year), while the third leg — Hyundai Motor preferreds — is the only place where the law's mechanics remain visibly *unpriced*: the pref-common discount **widened from ~30% at the start of 2026 to 56–58% today** because the AI/robotics melt-up chased the common. With the KOSPI down 18.5% from its June 19 peak, the won at a 17-year low, and foreigners dumping ₩34tn in ten sessions, this is a live but treacherous setup: the legal edge is real, but two of three legs are stale and the third is best bought on the unwind, not into it.

## 2. Who is selling and why (seller-identity analysis — the core of the edge)

**The question that matters: is the person selling you these securities better informed than you, or constrained?** By leg:

### (a) Hyundai preferreds — the seller is a *mandate*, not a mind
- The 2026 melt-up was driven by index/ETF flows, foreign momentum money and retail chasing the **robotics narrative in the common** (Boston Dynamics' electric Atlas humanoid began production Jan 5, 2026; BD is now marked ~₩30tn/$20bn — a 24x mark since 2021; Hyundai is buying out SoftBank's remaining BD stake, June 2026) ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/banking-finance/20260107/hyundai-motor-shares-soar-on-expectations-for-robotics-boom), [KED Global](https://www.kedglobal.com/robotics/newsView/ked202606210001)).
- KOSPI 200 ETFs exclude prefs; the passive wall of money bought common only. Seoul Economic Daily documented exactly this: Hyundai's three prefs' disparity ratios went from ~30% at the start of 2026 to **>60% by June** because the common rose ~130% YTD while prefs rose only 20–30%; analysts explicitly blamed ETF/index flows ([SED, June 5, 2026](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/06/05/preferred-shares-trade-at-steep-discounts-as-koreas-blue)). Nobody with information about Hyundai's cash flows "sold" the pref down to a 56% discount — the pref was simply *not bought*. That is a structural/mechanical mispricing, the best kind.
- Counter-signal worth noting: foreigners were reported *rotating into* Hyundai prefs as early as March ([SED, Mar 15, 2026](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/03/15/foreign-investors-shift-from-samsung-to-hyundai-preferred)) — smart money is on our side of this trade, which caps how "secret" it is.

### (b) The whole market this week — a forced, indiscriminate seller
Foreign funds are selling **Korea the country** (AI-capex doubt + won at 17-year lows creating a reflexive FX/equity doom loop). ₩34tn in ten sessions is country-level de-risking, not name-level information. Selling by someone hedging a currency and an index is the classic constrained counterparty — but it can persist for months, so it argues for staged entry, not absence.

### (c) The holdcos — the historic seller was right, and the law is what changed
SK Inc and Samsung C&T have been perpetual-discount vehicles because the marginal seller correctly believed controlling families would never return the trapped value: treasury stock was held as a control device (re-issuable to white knights), not cancelled. The 2025–26 legislative sequence attacked exactly this: (1) First Amendment (Jul 2025): directors' fiduciary duty extended to shareholders; (2) Second Amendment (promulgated Sep 9, 2025): cumulative voting / expanded separate election of audit committee members ([Kim & Chang](https://www.kimchang.com/en/insights/detail.kc?sch_section=4&idx=33096)); (3) **Third Amendment (Mar 6, 2026): treasury shares defined as rightless "unissued shares," mandatory cancellation within 1 year (18 months for legacy blocks), disposal only via shareholder-approved plans on equal/pro-rata terms, treasury-backed CBs and pledges banned** ([Kim & Chang](https://www.kimchang.com/en/insights/detail.kc?sch_section=4&idx=34499), [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/third-amendment-to-korean-commercial-code-on-mandatory-treasury-share-cancellation/)). The historic reason to sell holdcos cheap is being legislated away — but the market has already paid itself for much of this (see §5).

### (d) The informed sellers you should actually fear
- **SK Inc: the Chey divorce.** Chairman Chey Tae-won (17.9% personal; family + related parties ~25.5% as of Mar 2025) faces a ~₩1.38tn property division; court mediation **failed on June 15, 2026** ([UPI](https://www.upi.com/amp/Top_News/World-News/2026/06/15/korea-SK-Group-chairman-Chey-Tae-won-divorce-settlement-assets-court-mediation-no-agreement/9101781514893/)). Press has floated Siltron stake sales and share-backed loans. A controlling shareholder who may need to sell or pledge stock — and who arguably benefits from a *low* SK Inc price while assets are being divided and succession looms (Korea's 50–60% inheritance tax) — is a genuinely adverse counterparty.
- **Samsung C&T: family inheritance-tax sales.** The Lee family's installment plan (2021–2026) has driven periodic block sales across Samsung entities. Mostly done (final installments ~2026, unverified), but block-sale overhang is non-zero.
- **Hyundai: succession is the *buyer's* friend for now.** The family (Chung Euisun: 2.73% of HMC, 1.81% of Kia; Hyundai Mobis: 22.36% of HMC) currently *wants* HMC value high and share count shrinking — cancellations passively raise Mobis's grip and appreciate the chairman's stake ahead of an expected Mobis-holdco restructuring ([Daum/analyst piece, Apr 2026](https://v.daum.net/v/20260420102711108)). Alignment is positive *today*; the restructuring event itself is where prefs could be mistreated (see §7).

**Verdict on the edge:** the only leg where the current seller is demonstrably uninformed/mechanical is the Hyundai pref leg. For SK and C&T, the sellers of the last 12 months were, if anything, too slow — those stocks have already re-rated 135–205%.

## 3. Business quality & moat

- **Hyundai Motor (the asset behind the prefs):** A top-3 global automaker by volume with genuine hybrid/EV breadth, a US manufacturing footprint (Metaplant) partially mitigating tariffs, a captive finance arm, and — new since 2025 — a credible physical-AI/robotics option in Boston Dynamics (Atlas in production Jan 2026, externally marked ~$20bn). Autos are a mediocre business (cyclical, capital-hungry, ~6–7% operating margins guided for 2026), but Hyundai is a *good* operator of a mediocre business, with a formalized capital-return regime: ≥25% payout floor, quarterly dividends, TSR ≥35% target for 2025–27, and a ₩4tn buy-and-cancel program 2025–27 that **now cannot be warehoused as treasury — the law forces it into permanent share-count shrink** ([Hyundai IR](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/company/ir/stock-information/shareholder-return-policy)). The prefs are non-voting, non-cumulative participating perpetuals paying common DPS + ₩50–100 — economically the same claim at 44% of the price.
- **SK Inc:** A holding company whose NAV is now dominated by a single look-through asset: 31.5% of SK Square, which is >95% a 20.07% stake in SK hynix — the global HBM leader that overtook Samsung as Korea's most valuable company in the AI boom ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20251209/will-new-shareholding-rule-encourage-conglomerates-investments)). Hynix's moat (HBM process leadership) is real but ferociously cyclical; SK Inc is a double-discounted, levered derivative of it, plus SK Innovation (55.9%, post-E&S merger — refining/battery, structurally challenged), SK Telecom (30.6%, mature, post-data-breach), SK Biopharmaceuticals (64%), and unlisted Siltron/Pharmteco/Ecoplant. Standalone net debt ₩8.4tn (3Q25).
- **Samsung C&T:** The de facto holdco of Samsung group — 298.2mn shares of Samsung Electronics (~5%), 43.1% of Samsung Biologics (world-class CDMO; plus the 2025 spun-off Epis biosimilar holding), 19.3% of Samsung Life, 17.1% of Samsung SDS — wrapped around decent-but-dull construction/trading/fashion/resort operations (FY2025 net income ₩2.42tn). Moat is the portfolio, not the operations.

Quality ranking for our purposes: Hyundai pref (good operator + contractual-ish return policy + cheapest claim) > Samsung C&T (great assets, spent catalyst) > SK Inc (great asset at bubble marks, adverse family situation).

## 7. Pre-mortem: the three most likely ways this loses money — tripwires and kill criteria

**1. The melt-up unwinds and the "spread" closes downward.** KOSPI did +76% (2025) then +123% at the June 2026 peak — the 1987/1999 analogs were followed by −23% and −51% years. In an unwind, the pref discount narrows because the *common falls*, and the pref still loses 25–40% absolute; SK Inc's NAV (hynix-levered) could halve outright. **Tripwires:** KOSPI <6,500 with foreign selling still >₩1tn/day ⇒ do not add to SK/C&T at any discount until hynix consensus earnings are re-based; Hyundai 2P: falling *price* is a buy trigger (₩175,000) **only if** trailing DPS ≥₩10,000 is intact and Q2 auto OPM ≥5%. **Kill:** Hyundai cuts DPS below the ≥25%-payout floor or suspends the ₩4tn program → thesis void, exit.

**2. The won keeps breaking (USD investor loses even when the thesis works).** KRW 1,555.8 today, 17-year low, 1,600 forecast; a further 10% devaluation erases the base-case pref return in USD. **Tripwires:** KRW >1,650 accompanied by CDS widening or failed BOK smoothing → hedge 50–100% of KRW exposure (carry is the cost of staying); conversely KRW <1,450 removes the objection. Note the flip side: entering at a 17-year-low won means FX is potential *tailwind* from here — but only after it stops falling.

**3. The families take the value privately anyway.** Three specific mechanisms: (a) **Hyundai restructuring ratio abuse** — a Mobis-holdco conversion that sets merger/swap ratios off depressed pref *market* prices would crystallize the discount against us (Korean precedent for ratio games is rich: C&T/Cheil 2015). (b) **The loophole**: "mandatory" cancellation has an articles-of-incorporation escape hatch (employee comp / "business objectives" retention plans with annual shareholder approval — Kim & Chang, Sodali); a future conservative government could also soften enforcement (penalty is only ~₩50mn/director, unverified). (c) **SK-specific**: Chey's divorce/succession makes a *low* SK Inc price privately useful and could produce pledged-share supply. **Tripwires:** any Hyundai buyback tranche that excludes prefs (two consecutive = kill for the spread thesis); restructuring announcement using market-price-based pref treatment (kill; exit on the news pop if any); March 2027 AGM season shows >30% of large caps adopting retention plans (thesis-wide downgrade); disclosure of Chey share pledges/sales (avoid SK until absorbed).

## 8. Verdict & the price/fact that changes it

**Basket verdict: WATCH — and the basket construction itself is rejected.** An equal-weighted basket dilutes the one live edge (Hyundai prefs) with two spent catalysts (SK, C&T) and concentrates hidden factor risk: all three legs are long the same KOSPI/AI/won complex that is breaking *this week*. Single-name selection beats the basket.

- **Hyundai Motor 2nd pref (005387; 3P 005389 as a smaller, cheaper, less liquid kicker): conditional PURSUE.** Buy trigger: **₩175,000 on the 2P (≈5.7% trailing yield, ~5.3× normalized EPS, likely >60% discount)** — plausibly reachable within weeks at the current pace of the unwind — **or** any price ≤₩215,000 *after* the first 10-day stretch of foreign net buying with the discount still >52%. Half position at trigger, half after KOSPI stabilization. Exit/thesis-complete: discount <30% or the restructuring kill above.
- **SK Inc: WATCH.** The Jan 4, 2027 cancellation is real, dated and public — and therefore in the price at a 54% discount to a bubble-marked NAV, with a divorce overhang. Re-visit if (i) the stock falls to a >65% discount on a *post-correction* hynix mark, or (ii) the divorce overhang clears via a non-market solution.
- **Samsung C&T: PASS as a value-up cancellation trade** (the 13.2% block is mostly already retired — the backlog entry was stale on this); WATCH as a straight NAV play only after the Samsung Electronics AI re-rate mean-reverts.

**The single fact that flips the whole thing to PURSUE-now:** confirmation in the next Hyundai buyback tranche filing that preferreds are included pro-rata and cancelled — at a 56–58% discount, every won spent buying prefs destroys pref float at 44 cents on the common's dollar, mechanically compounding NAV/share for all classes and the family alike. **The fact that flips it to PASS:** a Mobis-holdco restructuring plan that converts prefs at market-based ratios, or Hyundai abandoning the ≥25% payout floor.
